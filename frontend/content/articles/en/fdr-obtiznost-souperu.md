**FDR** (fixture difficulty rating) is a simplified measure of how hard the upcoming opponent is. We use it in two places.

## In the team calendar

Each upcoming match has a weight from 1 (easiest) to 5 (hardest). It is calculated from the opponent's current league position, with band boundaries that differ depending on whether you play at home or away. An opponent from the top of the table is a 5, one from the bottom a 1.

## For players: performance by opponent

For players we split opponents into three groups based on last season's final table. A **tougher opponent** is the top five, **mid** is 6th to 11th place and **easier** is 12th place and below, including promoted teams.

This lets you see whether a striker is strong only against weaker sides or also performs against the top.

## Limitations

- Table position says little at the start of a season. For players we therefore use last year's table, but teams change.
- FDR knows nothing about injuries or form.
- Three bands are deliberately coarse. It is not an exact measure but a quick filter.
