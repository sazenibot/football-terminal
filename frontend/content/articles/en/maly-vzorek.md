At the start of a season there are few matches and every number moves a lot. A few rules of thumb that we use ourselves.

## Rules of thumb

- **Up to five matches:** treat differences as chance. One extra goal changes the average by a fifth.
- **Ten to twenty matches** already make sense for rough tendencies, not for exact percentages.
- **A whole season** is a better basis. That is why many statistics let you switch to all seasons.

## How we show it in the product

- A **small sample** label on referee comparisons with fewer than three matches.
- Players are ranked only among those who played at least 20% of possible minutes. Otherwise the top would be those who came on for ten minutes and scored.
- Clean sheets and similar percentages are always shown together with the number of matches.

## What to do about it

When a number surprises you, look at how many matches it comes from. If it is fewer than ten, treat it as a curiosity, not a conclusion.
