The site has three main parts. Each one answers a different question.

## Match Center: what is on and what to expect

The match list shows matches seven days ahead. Each has a mini model prediction and an icon showing whether the referee is already known. The match detail adds team playing styles, form, head-to-head, referee influence, odds and a simulation.

## Data catalog: who is who

Profiles of teams, players and referees across seasons. They are useful when you need context: how a team plays at home, how a player does against stronger opponents, or whether a referee gives more fouls to home teams.

## Results: does it work?

Every model prediction is locked before kick-off and evaluated after the match. The Results page has the live prediction ledger and a backtest. The numbers that do not work out for us are there too.

## How to read it together

1. Pick a match in the list.
2. In the detail, find out what the model expects and how it differs from the odds.
3. If you want to know why, open the team or player profile in the catalog.

Most numbers are explained by the “i” icon next to them. More detailed guides are in the other articles.
